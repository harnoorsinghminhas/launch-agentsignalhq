Card 1 · What SI means

Superintelligence (SI) is the new name for AI.

SI is short for superintelligence, the next generation of artificial intelligence (AI). Since Executive Order 14434, US federal agencies use the words "Super Intelligence" where they used to say "AI".

Remember: it is a new name. It does not mean machines now outthink people. You will still see both words, so we use both.

Card 2 · Three labels

Fact, widely reported, or rumor.

  • Fact Confirmed by the primary source: the company, the filing, the paper, the product you can try.
  • Widely reported Several outlets say it, but none shows the primary source yet.
  • Unconfirmed rumor One source, or none you can check. Interesting, not settled.
Card 3 · Your 30-second check

Three questions before you believe it.

  1. Who said it first? Find the original, not the retelling.
  2. Can you see it? A document, a demo, a filing, a dated post.
  3. Who confirms it? Someone with something to lose if it is wrong.

Remember: a rumor is not a lie. It is a claim waiting for evidence. Label it, then move on.
